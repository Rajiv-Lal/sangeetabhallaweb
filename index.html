Sangeeta Bhalla is a certified Ayurvedic practitioner, crystal healer, and meditation teacher with over two decades of practice. Her work bridges the ancient and the immediate — drawing from classical texts while remaining grounded in the lived experience of her clients.

She is the co-founder of Anugnya Holistic Care, a sanctuary for integrative wellness in Hyderabad, and the creator of the 3000 BC Therapeutics line of Ayurvedic formulations.

Her sessions are an invitation — not to fix, but to listen. To the body's intelligence. To the wisdom that already lives within you.
