Sangeeta Bhalla is a cancer survivor, energy healer, and aromatherapist based in Hyderabad. Her path into healing began with her own recovery from radical surgery — a journey in which therapeutic energy healing proved transformative. What started as a personal necessity became a decade-long study and practice that gave her the tools to heal herself, and eventually others.

She is the founder of 3000 BC Therapeutics and co-founder of Anugnya Holistic Care, where she works with cancer patients and caregivers to complement medical treatment with ancient healing wisdom.
